Three runs, same method.

- **Final run:** 2026-11-07, 19:49-20:01 UTC, on `m3-money` at `12600a9` (after fix rounds 2

  payments views and expenses.

  the routes the final run did not cover (`/admin`, fees), latency and the idle check.

inline: a throwaway `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`, `BETTER_AUTH_URL`, mail to the outbox.

`--extra-headers` with a fresh session-cache cookie for each run: residents' pages as `resident1`,
admin pages as `admin`. The `[id]` page is Apartment 12, as in M2. Every mobile route ran 3 times
(median reported) and desktop once.

**Machine load.** Every Lighthouse run waited for a 1-minute `vm.loadavg` below 10. The final run
ran at 4.4-9.8 (`.metrics/m3g/load.log`).

**E2e.** The full suite ran only on the first run: 3400, Neon test branch, observer-only Playwright
config because 3100 is taken. For the later runs the coordinator runs it as the gate. The final run
signed in three seeded users and signed them out through the app afterwards. It wrote nothing else.

## Misses

### 1. Mobile perf 93-94 on `/admin/payments` and `[id]`: the accepted admin LCP

These two pages miss the ≥ 95 performance budget in all 3 runs. Both misses come entirely from the
accepted admin-page LCP: TBT is 25-41 ms, CLS 0.029, and desktop is 100. Leave it accepted; **Must
fix** only if the user withdraws the acceptance.

**Measured (3 runs each):**

| Route             | Perf       | LCP                | FCP    | Document on the wire       | LCP element, render delay                                     |
| ----------------- | ---------- | ------------------ | ------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/admin/payments` | 93, 93, 93 | 3.17, 3.17, 3.17 s | 0.91 s | 36.1 kB (42 r1, 51 before) | header lead `header > p.text-text-muted` 380 × 48, 397-398 ms |
| `[id]`            | 94, 94, 94 | 3.09, 3.11, 3.09 s | 1.05 s | 45.3-46.9 kB (53.8 r1)     | invite card lead `section.rounded-card > p` 330 × 59, 368 ms  |
| `?year=2025`      | 96, 94, 96 | 2.79, 3.02, 2.79 s | 0.91 s | 35.5 kB                    | header lead, 353-408 ms                                       |
| `?apartment=`     | 96, 94, 95 | 2.86, 3.11, 2.86 s | 1.05 s | 44.4 kB (51.0 r1)          | header lead                                                   |
| expenses          | 93, 95, 95 | 3.17, 2.96, 2.94 s | 0.90 s | 39.3 kB (47.9 r1)          | header lead 380 × 72, 385-402 ms                              |

**Cause:**

- **The LCP element streams in after the role check.** On every admin page the LCP element is in
  the page's `<Suspense>` boundary, behind `requireRole`. This is the accepted admin-LCP cause.
  Unthrottled, it paints at 377-415 ms on every admin page here.
- **Why payments now reads 3.17 s and `?year=` 2.79 s.** Lighthouse's simulation (Lantern) puts the
  LCP on one of a few discrete steps (~2.79, ~3.0, ~3.17 s), and the two views land on different
  steps for the same page shape.
  - **The two documents stream identically:** shell at 7-10 ms, nav at ~145 ms, the page at
    ~290 ms, chunks of the same sizes (`.metrics/m3g/chunks.mjs`).
  - **They make the same 67-69 requests,** including the same 31 sign-in-redirected prefetches as
    in round 1 (an artifact of signing in by `--extra-headers`, present in every run).
  - **They swapped places between runs.** In round 1, payments read 2.80 s and `?year=` 3.01 s.
  - **Verdict:** this is not a regression from rounds 2-3, but the number is what Lighthouse gives.
- **FCP step on `[id]` and `?apartment=`.** Their documents (44.4-46.9 kB on the wire) still take
  one more simulated round trip before FCP (1.05 s against 0.90 s). Expenses dropped from 47.9 to
  39.3 kB and lost the step: FCP 0.90 s, perf 95.
  - **Corrected threshold:** it lies between 39.3 and 44.4 kB, not "~45-48 kB" as round 1 said.

**Fix if not accepted (ranked by impact):**

1. **Paint the admin page header from the static shell.** This is the pattern `12600a9` used for
   Money and Balance: move each admin page's title and lead into a segment layout, or above the
   `<Suspense>`.
   - **Effect:** LCP would drop to about FCP (~0.9 s) on every admin page, as it did on `/money`
     (2.80 → 0.91 s).
   - **Trade-off:** today the pages deliberately reveal nothing to non-members before the role
     check (`app/[locale]/(app)/admin/payments/page.tsx:9-11`). The header copy would reach anyone
     who opens the URL before the 404 content streams in. That is a product decision, not just
     performance.
2. **`[id]` only:** get its document under ~40 kB for FCP 0.90 s. Expenses at 39.3 kB did this. A
   cap of 3 instead of 5 payments, or fewer invite-card strings in the client messages, would
   likely be enough. This would probably bring `[id]` to 95-96, not to 100, while the LCP stays
   behind the role check.
